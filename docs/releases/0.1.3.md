MangoMemory now updates itself.

## Added

- **Check for Updates…** in the gear menu, and **Check for updates automatically** in Settings › General. When a new version is out, MangoMemory shows what changed, downloads it, checks its signature, and installs it in place. Your settings and the helper approval carry over.
- If a daily check finds an update while you're busy, the gear in the memory window gets a small dot until you look at it.

## Privacy

The update check is the only new network use: once a day, MangoMemory downloads the list of releases from mangocorporation.github.io. It sends nothing else, and you can turn it off in Settings › General.
